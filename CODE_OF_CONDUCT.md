# Code of conduct

ClassSync welcomes constructive, respectful contributions. Discuss ideas and code on their merits. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. To report conduct privately, email `hello@hugoalmeida.tech` with links and context. For vulnerabilities, use [private security reporting](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports will be handled discreetly, with no promise of a fixed response time.
